A product for chronic ADHD should externalize executive function — acting as a "second brain" that compensates for working memory deficits, time blindness, task initiation paralysis, and emotional overwhelm. Below are concrete, research-backed feature ideas organized by the specific ADHD challenge each one solves. [healthline](https://www.healthline.com/health/adhd/how-to-cope-with-executive-dysfunction)

## Core Challenges to Target

ADHD executive dysfunction manifests as difficulty with planning, prioritization, time estimation, task initiation, sustained attention, organization, and emotional regulation. A successful product doesn't just replicate a neurotypical to-do list — it actively scaffolds these weakened cognitive processes. [nextsteppsychiatry](https://nextsteppsychiatry.com/blog/adhd-executive-dysfunction-guide)

## Time Blindness & Scheduling

- **Timeboxing view** — Assign every task to a visual time block rather than an endless list; this combats distorted time perception by making duration tangible [adhdbright](https://adhdbright.com/shop/adhd-planner/)
- **Estimate-vs-actual comparison** — Let users guess how long a task will take, then log the real time; repeated feedback retrains time awareness [adhdbright](https://adhdbright.com/shop/adhd-planner/)
- **Visual day timeline** — A horizontal bar showing the full day with colored task blocks, transitions, and gaps so the user can literally "see" their day at a glance [play.google](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mycompany.adhdplannerfocus)
- **Smart gentle reminders** — Non-guilt nudge alerts that fire before transitions, deadlines, and appointments; multiple staggered reminders per event rather than a single alarm [nextsteppsychiatry](https://nextsteppsychiatry.com/blog/adhd-executive-dysfunction-guide)

## Task Initiation & Breakdown

- **Automatic micro-step decomposition** — User enters a big task ("Do taxes"), and the app auto-generates absurdly small first steps ("Open laptop → Go to portal → Download form"); ADHD brains need the path mapped, not just the destination [nextsteppsychiatry](https://nextsteppsychiatry.com/blog/adhd-executive-dysfunction-guide)
- **"What's the next action?" prompt** — A persistent single-action focus that surfaces only the very next physical step, hiding everything else to reduce overwhelm [amazon](https://www.amazon.com/ADHD-Daily-Planner-Neurodivergent-Adults/dp/B0CG78SLJL)
- **One-tap start** — Reduce friction to beginning a task; the fewer taps between "I should do this" and "I'm doing this," the higher the initiation rate [habi](https://habi.app/insights/how-to-build-habits-with-adhd/)

## Prioritization & Overwhelm

- **Top-3-only daily view** — Force the user to pick a maximum of three priorities; everything else goes to a "later" parking lot [amazon](https://www.amazon.com/ADHD-Daily-Planner-Neurodivergent-Adults/dp/B0CG78SLJL)
- **Brain dump capture zone** — A frictionless inbox where swirling thoughts, reminders, and distractions can be dumped instantly and triaged later when the user is ready [apps.apple](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/focused-space-get-more-done/id6755700428)
- **Energy-based task matching** — Let users tag tasks by energy required (low/medium/high) and filter by current energy level, since ADHD motivation fluctuates dramatically [play.google](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mycompany.adhdplannerfocus)

## Motivation & Reward (Dopamine Scaffolding)

- **Immediate micro-rewards on completion** — Confetti, sounds, points, or visual progress bars that fire the instant a task is checked off; ADHD brains need amplified, immediate dopamine signals [affine](https://affine.pro/blog/adhd-friendly-habit-tracker-ideas)
- **Forgiving streaks** — Missing a day never resets a habit streak; this prevents the all-or-nothing abandonment cycle common in ADHD [adhdbright](https://adhdbright.com/shop/adhd-planner/)
- **Progress visualization** — Show cumulative wins, completed micro-steps, and patterns over time so the user can see growth rather than focusing on what's undone [play.google](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mycompany.adhdplannerfocus)
- **Gamified reverse to-do list** — Instead of an intimidating growing list, show a "done" list that fills up, turning completion into the primary visual [blink](https://blink.new/case-studies/reverse-todo-adhd-app-ubphpcvu)

## Focus & Sustained Attention

- **Built-in body doubling** — Ambient presence features (virtual co-working rooms, subtle soundscapes, or peer sessions) that create the feeling of someone working beside the user; body doubling is a proven ADHD focus technique [apps.apple](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/beside-body-doubling-focus/id6759262598)
- **Pomodoro / flow timer with rewards** — Timed focus sessions (15/25/45 min) with gentle completion chimes and optional breaks; consistency is rewarded, not perfection [play.google](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mycompany.adhdplannerfocus)
- **Distraction blocker integration** — Grayscale mode toggles, notification batching, and app-blocking during focus sessions to reduce dopamine hijacking from the phone itself [brain](https://www.brain.fm/blog/digital-minimalism-adhd-phone-focus)

## Working Memory & Follow-Through

- **Externalized capture-first workflow** — Every thought, commitment, or idea goes into the system immediately rather than relying on memory; the app becomes the user's working memory [nextsteppsychiatry](https://nextsteppsychiatry.com/blog/adhd-executive-dysfunction-guide)
- **Contextual re-surfacing** — Instead of a stagnant list, the app intelligently re-surfaces the right task at the right time based on location, time of day, or current context [habi](https://habi.app/insights/how-to-build-habits-with-adhd/)
- **Auto-rollover of unfinished tasks** — Uncompleted tasks carry forward automatically so nothing falls through the cracks; the user doesn't have to manually migrate [apps.apple](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/focused-space-get-more-done/id6755700428)

## Routines & Habits

- **Pre-built routine checklists** — Morning, evening, and wellness routines come pre-loaded so the user doesn't have to design systems from scratch [adhdbright](https://adhdbright.com/shop/adhd-planner/)
- **Visual routine builders** — Drag-and-drop routine creation with icons and colors that match how the ADHD brain processes visual information better than text [nextsteppsychiatry](https://nextsteppsychiatry.com/blog/adhd-executive-dysfunction-guide)

## Emotional Regulation & Self-Awareness

- **Guided daily reflection prompts** — Short, structured questions ("What gave you energy today? What drained it?") that build metacognition without being journaling-heavy [adhdbright](https://adhdbright.com/shop/adhd-planner/)
- **Mood/energy logging** — Lightweight emoji-based check-ins that help users identify patterns between their state and productivity [play.google](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mycompany.adhdplannerfocus)
- **Compassionate, non-judgmental tone** — All copy should avoid shame language ("You missed 3 tasks!") in favor of encouraging framing ("Good start — here's what's next") [apps.apple](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/focused-space-get-more-done/id6755700428)

## Design Principles for the ADHD Brain

- **Low cognitive load per screen** — Show one primary action at a time; hide secondary options behind progressive disclosure [someclaudeskills](https://someclaudeskills.com/docs/skills/adhd_design_expert/)
- **High visual contrast & color coding** — Use color, icons, and spatial layout to convey priority and category rather than relying on reading and sorting [nextsteppsychiatry](https://nextsteppsychiatry.com/blog/adhd-executive-dysfunction-guide)
- **Minimal onboarding friction** — No lengthy setup; the user should be productive within 60 seconds of opening the app for the first time [apps.apple](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/beside-body-doubling-focus/id6759262598)
- **Flexibility over rigidity** — The system must adapt to fluctuating energy and motivation rather than demanding consistency; allow mid-day replanning without penalty [play.google](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mycompany.adhdplannerfocus)

***

The highest-impact combination for a new product would likely be **automatic task micro-decomposition + timeboxing + forgiving dopamine rewards + integrated body doubling** — these four features directly attack the four most disabling ADHD challenges (initiation paralysis, time blindness, motivation deficit, and sustained attention) in a way that most existing planners address only partially.
