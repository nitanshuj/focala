# FoCala — How the App Helps Your Brain Get Things Done

Welcome! This document explains in simple, plain language how the **FoCala backend system** works to support anyone dealing with ADHD, working memory challenges, executive dysfunction, or daily task overwhelm.

---

## 🌟 The Core Mission
Standard to-do list apps often fail ADHD brains because endless lists feel overwhelming, time is hard to estimate, and starting a big project causes freeze mode. 

FoCala acts as a **smart digital assistant (your external second brain)**. It breaks down big scary tasks into tiny steps, organizes your day automatically, sends non-guilty reminders, and rewards your daily progress.

---

## 🚀 Key Features Explained (What We Built)

### 1. Smart AI Daily Planner 📅
- **How it works:** Every morning, the app looks at your tasks, your energy levels, and your routines. It creates a simple, balanced daily schedule.
- **Why it helps:** 
  - Never schedules more than **3 big focus tasks** a day to prevent burnout.
  - Automatically adds **15-minute rest buffers** between focus blocks.
  - Includes **flex blocks** so if your day gets off track, you can easily recover without guilt.

### 2. Task Breakdown Assistant (Micro-Steps) 🧩
- **How it works:** When a task feels too big (like *"Clean the house"* or *"Do taxes"*), you tap **Break Down Task**. The AI breaks it into 3 to 5 tiny, friction-free physical actions (like *"1. Pick up 3 items off the floor"* or *"2. Open tax website"*).
- **Why it helps:** Eliminates "task initiation paralysis." You only ever have to focus on the absolute first physical step.

### 3. Brain Dump & Thoughts Inbox 🧠
- **How it works:** When random ideas, chores, or worries pop into your mind, you can type them out into a quick **Brain Dump**. Later, our AI triages your notes and converts them into organized tasks with priority level and estimated time.
- **Why it helps:** Frees up space in your working memory instantly so you don't forget things or feel distracted while working.

### 4. Automatic Unfinished Task Rollover 🔄
- **How it works:** Any task you didn't finish yesterday automatically carries over to today's list without you having to manually drag or rewrite it.
- **Why it helps:** Keeps unfinished items from slipping through the cracks and removes the shame of having uncompleted tasks from yesterday.

### 5. Reverse To-Do List & Micro-Rewards (Dopamine Booster) 🏆
- **How it works:** Instead of staring at a scary long list of remaining tasks, FoCala tracks your **Done List**. The moment you complete a task or micro-step, you get instant rewards, streak progress, and positive visual feedback.
- **Why it helps:** Gives your brain instant positive feedback (dopamine) every time you take action.

### 6. Body Doubling & Virtual Focus Rooms 👥
- **How it works:** Includes virtual focus sessions and ambient soundscapes (like *Quiet Library*, *Coffee Shop*, or *Rain Sounds*). 
- **Why it helps:** Having a gentle background presence makes it much easier to stay focused and feel accompanied while working.

### 7. Gentle Push Reminders & Routine Trackers 🔔
- **How it works:** Sends non-judgmental nudge notifications before transition times, focus blocks, and evening check-ins. It also tracks your morning and evening routines.
- **Why it helps:** Missing a habit or routine day never resets your streak to zero. The app stays compassionate and forgiving.

### 8. Energy & Mood Check-Ins ⚡
- **How it works:** Quick emoji check-ins let you log how energetic or overwhelmed you feel. The planner uses this data to recommend low-energy tasks when you feel tired and high-energy tasks when you feel sharp.
- **Why it helps:** Adapts your day to how you actually feel rather than forcing rigid schedules.
